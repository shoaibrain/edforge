Saraswati English Boarding School \- Private Boarding School 

The Counselling Department serves all students through a systematic, sequential, clearly defined, and accountable program.  
School counselors meet the needs of students based on data, alignment with district goals, and a unified belief in the mission and vision of the SSSEBS.

The program is delivered by experience & Professional school counselors in collaboration with faculty, staff, families, and the community. It is aligned with the Nepal Model for Comprehensive School Counseling Programs which integrates Academic, Social-Emotional, and Career Development so that students will be able to demonstrate competencies in interpersonal and intrapersonal effectiveness, college planning and career readiness, and personal health and safety.

# **Academic Planning**

SSEBS offers Academic Planning to assist students in experiencing a rich and rewarding educational career. Below are resources to assist students in exploring opportunities for their secondary school experience. 

1. Academic Planning Guide  
   The Academic Planning Guide is a course catalog consisting of course offerings, course descriptions, course weights and other relative course information including graduation requirements. This guide is to assist students in course planning during their secondary school experience. There are Academic Planning Guides for both Elementary School,middle school and Lowery high School (Grade 8 \- Grade 10, Graduates with SEE).  
     
    Please note that Academic Planning Guides vary by academic year and graduating class, which can affect course weights and course codes.  
     
   Elementary School Academic Planning Guide  
   Middle School Academic Planning Guide  
     
2. Course Selection  
   In the 8th grade, students meet with counselors to develop a personal graduation plan for SEE (Formerly SLC). During course selection, students identify the courses they need in order to meet that graduation plan. For middle school course selection, students are provided a preview of core course requirements as well as elective options. Students then make selections based on a combination of required courses and those they wish to explore as electives.
