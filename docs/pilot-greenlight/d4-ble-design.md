# D.4.0 Research — BLE (Grade 8) Workflow Design

## 1. Authority & Standards

Under Nepal's federalized structure, the authority to conduct the Basic Level Examination (BLE, formerly DLE) is exclusively decentralized to local governments (Municipalities and Metropolitan Cities).

* **1.1 Legal Basis:** The Constitution of Nepal delegates the jurisdiction of basic and secondary education to local governments. Consequently, municipalities are responsible for developing local education policies, conducting the Grade 8 exams, and publishing results. *(Source: [Local Education Policies in Nepal - NCE Nepal](https://ncenepal.org.np/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Local-Education-Policies-in-Nepal-1.pdf))*
* **1.2 Standards:** Despite local administration, municipalities must align with the national framework mandated by the Curriculum Development Centre (CDC) under the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST). *(Source: [Basic Education Curriculum Grades 6-8 - CDC](https://lib.moecdc.gov.np/elibrary/pages/download.php?direct=1&noattach=true&ref=9952&ext=pdf&k=))*
* **1.3 Question Paper Provenance:** The municipality's Education Department (or a designated Examination Conduct and Coordination Committee) drafts and distributes the final BLE question papers to ensure uniformity across all schools within that municipality. *(Source: [Only 33 percent of students passed grade eight exams in Dhangadhi - Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/sudurpaschim-province/2023/04/24/only-33-percent-of-students-passed-grade-eight-exams-in-dhangadhi-this-year))*
* **1.4 Answer Sheet Marking:** Grading is centrally managed by the municipality to prevent school-level grade inflation. Answer sheets are submitted to the municipal Education Department or designated resource centers for blind evaluation. *(Source: [LMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/lmc-published-ble-result/))*
* **1.5 Municipal Procedures:** Both Kathmandu Metropolitan City (KMC) and Lalitpur Metropolitan City (LMC) form specific steering committees chaired by local officials (like the Deputy Mayor) to oversee exam conduct, re-totaling, and supplementary exams. *(Source: [KMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/kmc-published-ble-result/))*

## 2. Registration Process

The registration workflow acts as the official census of Grade 8 students eligible to sit for the municipal exams.

* **2.1 Registration Flow:** Schools must register their Grade 8 cohort using the national Integrated Educational Management Information System (IEMIS) portal. Municipalities pull the roster data from IEMIS to generate exam symbol numbers and allocate centers. *(Source: [LMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/lmc-published-ble-result/))*
* **2.2 Required Data:** Standard student-level data includes: Full Name, Date of Birth (in BS), Gender, Parent/Guardian Names, School Name, School IEMIS Code, and the list of Compulsory and Optional Subjects.
* **2.3 Deadline:** Registrations are generally finalized in Magh or early Falgun (January/February), allowing municipalities roughly 4–6 weeks to arrange exam centers and print question papers before the Chaitra (March/April) exams.
* **2.4 Fees:** Exam fees are nominally set by the municipality. While exact baseline registration fees vary locally and are often waived for public schools, specific actions like re-totaling carry fixed fees (e.g., KMC charges Rs. 200 per subject for re-totaling). *(Source: [KMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/kmc-published-ble-result/))*
* **2.5 Caps:** There is no per-school cap; every enrolled Grade 8 student must be registered.

## 3. Admit Card

The admit card is the critical credential required for a student to enter the BLE examination hall.

* **3.1 Issuer:** The municipality generates the official symbol numbers and digital admit card records, which are then printed and distributed by the school administration.
* **3.2 Format & Fields:** Standard BLE admit cards include:
* Municipality Name & Logo
* Exam Year (e.g., BLE 2082)
* Symbol Number / Roll Number
* Student's Full Name & Date of Birth
* School Name & IEMIS Code
* Designated Exam Center
* List of Subjects (Compulsory + Local/Optional)
* Passport-size photograph of the student
* Signatures: School Principal (Headmaster) and the Municipal Education Officer.


* **3.5 Issuance Timing:** Admit cards are typically distributed to students 1 to 2 weeks prior to the start of the exams in Chaitra. *(Source: [KMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/kmc-published-ble-result/))*

## 4. Internal Assessment (CDC 50/50 Rubric)

Nepal's current Basic Level Curriculum emphasizes continuous evaluation over a single high-stakes test.

* **4.1 The 50/50 Rubric:** For Grade 8, the CDC mandates a strict 50% Internal Assessment (IA) and 50% External Evaluation (EE) weighting.
* **4.2 Breakdown:** The 50% Internal Assessment is driven by the Continuous Assessment System (CAS). It comprises Unit Tests, Project Works, Book Reviews, Field Trip Reports, and class participation. *(Source: [Little Angels' School Prospectus](https://las.edu.np/prospectus.pdf))*
* **4.3 Submission:** Schools must calculate the final out-of-50 internal assessment marks for every student and submit these ledgers to the municipality (often via the IEMIS portal or physical sealed ledgers) *before* the external written exams commence.
* **4.4 Deadlines:** Internal marks are locked in late Falgun, right before the Chaitra external exams. Municipalities merge these internal marks with the blind-graded external 50% to compute the final GPA.

## 5. External Exam

The external component represents the remaining 50% of the student's grade and is treated with high-stakes security.

* **5.1 Location:** Students rarely take the exam at their own school. Municipalities group schools into "Resource Centers" or designated examination centers to ensure impartial invigilation. *(Source: [Only 33 percent of students passed grade eight exams in Dhangadhi - Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/sudurpaschim-province/2023/04/24/only-33-percent-of-students-passed-grade-eight-exams-in-dhangadhi-this-year))*
* **5.2 Subjects Tested:** The standard exam schedule spans 7 to 8 days, covering: English, Nepali, Science & Technology, Mathematics, Social Studies & Human Value Education, Health/Physical & Creative Arts, and a Local Subject. *(Source: [KMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/kmc-published-ble-result/))*
* **5.3 Supervision:** Invigilation is cross-managed. Teachers from School A are often assigned to supervise students at Exam Center B to prevent cheating and maintain exam integrity.

## 6. Result Delivery

Result publication is a centralized digital event managed by the municipality and the central IEMIS infrastructure.

* **6.1 Delivery Mechanism:** Municipalities publish the official results digitally. Schools must log into the federal IEMIS portal (emis.cehrd.gov.np) to download the "Grade-wise Ledger" for their specific cohort. *(Source: [KMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/kmc-published-ble-result/))*
* **6.2 Latency:** Exams typically conclude in mid-Chaitra (e.g., Chaitra 12). Results are published roughly 3–4 weeks later, in early to mid-Baishakh.
* **6.3 Format:** The ledger provides a per-subject breakdown using a Letter Grade system (A+, A, B+, B, C+, C, D, NG) alongside the accumulated GPA. The "NG" (Not Graded) designation is applied to any subject where the student fails to secure the minimum required threshold (usually 35% in the external theoretical component).

## 7. Promotion & Re-exam

The transition from Grade 8 to Grade 9 involves specific academic safety nets.

* **7.1 Promotion Rules:** While the BLE is a gateway exam, a strict "fail" does not permanently bar a student from education. However, students receiving an NG must clear the supplementary exams to officially register as regular Grade 9 students in the municipal ledger.
* **7.2 Grade Increment (Supplementary) Exams:** Municipalities schedule supplementary exams very shortly after result publication. For example, KMC scheduled 2082 supplementary exams from Baishakh 6 to Baishakh 12. Students who receive an NG in up to *three* subjects are eligible to sit for these re-exams. *(Source: [KMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/kmc-published-ble-result/))*
* **7.3 Remedial Support:** Schools are expected to run intensive remedial crash courses between the result publication and the supplementary exam dates to support NG students.

## 8. IEMIS Portal Interaction

The Integrated Educational Management Information System (IEMIS) is the digital backbone of Nepal's public education administration.

* **8.1 Portal Usage:** School administrators log into emis.cehrd.gov.np using their unique 9-digit school IEMIS code.
* **8.2 BLE Submissions:** The portal is used to upload the initial Grade 8 student roster (registration), submit internal assessment (CAS) marks, and eventually download the official Grade-wise ledger (results) exported by the municipality. *(Source: [LMC Published BLE Result - Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/lmc-published-ble-result/))*

## 9. Allen ISD STAAR — Structural Reference

For architectural inspiration, we look to how Allen ISD (Texas) manages the State of Texas Assessments of Academic Readiness (STAAR) workflow.

* **9.1 Registration Workflow:** In Allen ISD, the state (TEA) mandates the exam, but the district manages logistics. The district's SIS (Skyward) maintains the definitive student roster. Prior to the testing window, Allen ISD extracts an authoritative enrollment file from Skyward and uploads it to the state testing vendor (Cambium/TIDE).
* **9.2 Admit Card Analog:** Texas does not use physical "Admit Cards" with photos for middle schoolers. Instead, the analog is the "Testing Ticket"—a secure slip generated by the testing portal containing the student's legal name, state ID, and the secure login credentials required to access the digital STAAR test on their Chromebooks.
* **9.3 Structural Takeaway:** The EMIS (Skyward/EdForge) is the source of truth for demographics and course enrollments. It must seamlessly format and export this data to meet the ingestion requirements of the external testing authority (Cambium/IEMIS).

## 11. EdForge Engineering Recommendations

Based on this workflow, EdForge must act as the ultimate staging ground for IEMIS compliance while providing a smooth UX for PABSON school administrators.

**1. Entity Shape: ExternalExamRegistration (BLE)**
This maps Ed-Fi's StudentAssessmentRegistration concept to the Nepal context.

```typescript
interface BleRegistration {
  id: string;                      // PK
  tenantId: string;                // SK (School)
  studentId: string;               // FK to Student
  academicYear: string;            // e.g., "2082"
  municipalityId: string;          // FK to Municipality lookup
  symbolNumber?: string;           // Assigned post-registration by municipality
  examCenter?: string;             // Assigned post-registration
  subjects: string[];              // Array of Course IDs
  status: 'DRAFT' | 'SUBMITTED_TO_IEMIS' | 'SYMBOL_ASSIGNED';
}

```

**2. Entity Shape: InternalAssessment (CDC 50% Rubric)**
Must track the specific CAS sub-components before rolling up to a final out-of-50 score.

```typescript
interface BleInternalAssessment {
  id: string;
  tenantId: string;
  registrationId: string;          // FK to BleRegistration
  courseId: string;                // FK to Course (e.g., Science 8)
  marks: {
    unitTests: number;             // Max varies, scaled
    projectWork: number;
    participation: number;
    totalInternal: number;         // Max 50
  };
  status: 'DRAFT' | 'LOCKED_FOR_IEMIS';
}

```

**3. Entity Shape: ExternalExamResult**

```typescript
interface BleResult {
  id: string;
  tenantId: string;
  registrationId: string;
  courseId: string;
  externalMark: number;            // Out of 50
  totalMark: number;               // Internal + External
  letterGrade: string;             // A+, A, B+, etc., or 'NG'
  gradePoint: number;              // 4.0 scale
  isSupplementary: boolean;        // True if achieved via Grade Increment exam
}

```

**4. Endpoint Design & Workflow Integration**

* **POST /api/ble/register-cohort:** Pulls all active Grade 8 enrollments and bulk-creates BleRegistration records.
* **GET /api/ble/export-iemis-csv:** Generates the precise CSV format required by emis.cehrd.gov.np for bulk student registration and internal mark submission.
* **POST /api/ble/import-iemis-ledger:** Parses the Grade-wise Ledger downloaded from IEMIS to bulk-update symbolNumber, examCenter, and eventually BleResult records.
* **GET /api/ble/admit-cards.pdf:** Triggers a server-side PDF generation job (using Puppeteer or PDFKit) for the cohort.

**5. Admit-Card PDF Template Field List**
The Handlebars template for the PDF must render:

* School Logo (Top Left) & Municipality Logo (Top Right)
* Header: "[Municipality Name] Basic Level Examination (BLE) - [Year BS]"
* Student Photo Box (Right-aligned)
* Student Name, DOB (BS), Gender
* Symbol Number (Huge font)
* School Name & IEMIS Code
* Exam Center Name
* Table of Subjects (Compulsory vs. Optional)
* Signature Lines: Student, Headmaster (with school seal placeholder), Municipal Officer.

**6. Per-Municipality Variation Handling Strategy**
**Recommendation: One Schema, Configurable Exports.**
Do *not* create separate database tables for Kathmandu vs. Lalitpur. The core data (Student, Internal Marks out of 50, Subject List) is identical because it is mandated federally by the CDC. The variations lie entirely in *formatting* and *deadlines*. Handle variation at the presentation layer:

* Use a MunicipalityConfig JSON object on the Tenant record to store specific municipality names, logos, and regional IEMIS export header mappings.

**7. IEMIS Portal Integration Strategy**
**Recommendation: CSV Export/Import (Manual Transit).**
The Nepal IEMIS portal does not currently expose a secure, public-facing REST API for third-party SaaS platforms to push data directly. EdForge must generate meticulously formatted Excel/CSV files that the school administrator manually downloads from EdForge and uploads to emis.cehrd.gov.np. Conversely, results must be ingested by allowing the admin to upload the IEMIS ledger PDF/Excel back into EdForge.

## 12. Open Questions / Confidence Caveats

* **Exact IEMIS CSV Column Headers:** While we know IEMIS accepts digital uploads, the exact column mapping (e.g., is Date of Birth expected as YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY/MM/DD in BS?) is not published in public documentation. *Needs internal decision based on best-available evidence (requires acquiring a sample IEMIS template from a partner school).*
* **Standardized vs. Custom Internal Assessment Sub-categories:** The CDC mandates a 50% internal weight, but whether IEMIS requires the exact breakdown (Unit test vs. Project) or just the final /50 aggregate score is unclear from public municipal notices. *Needs internal decision: Safest approach is to track granularly in EdForge but only export the aggregate if IEMIS rejects wide tables.*
