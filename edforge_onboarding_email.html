Getting Started — What to Do First
1
Log in and change your password
Use the temporary password above. You will be prompted to create a secure new password immediately.
2
Confirm your workspace settings
Go to Settings → Workspace and verify your currency, timezone, and calendar system are correctly configured for your organization.
3
Set up your academic year
Go to Settings → Organization to create your first academic year and configure terms or semesters.
4
Create your first school
Add your school(s) under your organization. Each school can have its own grade ranges, schedules, and staff assignments.
5
Invite your staff and start operating
Add teachers, administrators, and staff from People → Staff Directory. Once staff are set up, you can begin enrollment, scheduling, and finance operations.
